# NUMMI Brand System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Product category:** Financial literacy and money-management application for children  
**Primary market:** Indonesia  
**Audience:** Children from kindergarten through Grade 9, parents, and schools  

---

## 1. Brand Foundation

### 1.1 Brand Name

# **nummi**

Use **nummi** as the primary consumer-facing wordmark.

Use **NUMMI** when:

- explaining the acronym;
- writing legal or corporate documents;
- displaying the name in small uppercase labels;
- creating formal school or partnership materials.

### 1.2 Brand Meaning

> **NUMMI — Numbuhi Uang lewat Misi, Manajemen, dan Investasi**

Letter structure:

| Letter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **N** | Numbuhi |
| **U** | Uang |
| **M** | Misi |
| **M** | Manajemen |
| **I** | Investasi |

The phrase communicates that children do not only collect money. They learn to give each rupiah a purpose, build habits through missions, manage decisions, and understand how money can grow.

### 1.3 Primary Tagline

> **Uang kecil, kebiasaan besar.**

### 1.4 Supporting Taglines

- **Setiap rupiah punya tujuan.**
- **Belajar uang sambil bertumbuh.**
- **Pakai. Simpan. Berbagi. Bertumbuh.**
- **Little money, big lessons.**
- **Grow money habits together.**

### 1.5 Brand Promise

> Nummi helps children understand money through small decisions they can see, practice, and discuss with their parents.

### 1.6 Brand Purpose

Membantu anak membangun hubungan yang sehat dengan uang sebelum memiliki rekening bank sendiri.

### 1.7 Brand Vision

Menjadi platform pembelajaran finansial anak yang paling relevan, aman, dan menyenangkan di Indonesia.

### 1.8 Brand Mission

Nummi teaches children to:

1. use money intentionally;
2. save for meaningful goals;
3. share with empathy;
4. understand how money can grow;
5. learn through missions and real-life practice;
6. discuss financial decisions with parents.

## 2. Brand Positioning

### 2.1 Positioning Statement

> **Nummi adalah teman belajar keuangan untuk anak yang membantu mereka memakai, menyimpan, berbagi, dan menumbuhkan uang melalui misi serta pendampingan orang tua.**

### 2.2 What Nummi Is

- A financial-learning companion.
- A parent-and-child money ledger.
- A habit-building platform.
- A bridge between financial concepts and real-life actions.
- A school-friendly financial literacy ecosystem.

### 2.3 What Nummi Is Not

- A real bank account.
- An investment platform.
- A digital wallet holding real funds.
- A shopping application.
- A game that rewards children for accumulating the most money.
- A simplified adult banking dashboard.

### 2.4 Core Product Principle

> **Every rupiah has one home and one purpose.**

The product model must always communicate:

`Unsorted + Spend + Save + Give + Grow = Total`

## 3. Brand Personality

Nummi should feel like:

| Trait | Meaning in practice |
|---|---|
| **Warm** | Friendly, encouraging, never intimidating |
| **Curious** | Invites children to explore choices |
| **Clear** | Explains money without jargon |
| **Playful** | Uses illustration, motion, and stories |
| **Responsible** | Does not trivialize financial decisions |
| **Optimistic** | Treats mistakes as opportunities to learn |
| **Growing** | Celebrates progress and consistency |
| **Trustworthy** | Clearly separates app records from real money |

### 3.1 Personality Spectrum

| Avoid | Nummi | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Babyish | Playful but smart | Corporate |
| Chaotic | Expressive but structured | Sterile |
| Preachy | Educational through action | Entertainment-only |
| Competitive | Progress-oriented | Punitive |
| Financial jargon | Clear financial language | Oversimplified claims |

## 4. Logo System

## 4.1 Primary Wordmark

The primary logo is a custom lowercase wordmark:

# **nummi**

Lowercase lettering makes the brand:

- more approachable for children;
- friendlier in app interfaces;
- less similar to a financial institution;
- easier to combine with a mascot.

### 4.2 Wordmark Design Direction

Recommended letter characteristics:

- rounded geometric forms;
- soft terminals;
- medium-to-bold weight;
- generous internal spacing;
- no sharp corners;
- distinctive but highly legible.

Suggested custom details:

- The **u** can resemble a small container or pocket.
- The double **m** can form two gentle hills, representing gradual growth.
- The dot of the **i** can become a coin, seed, star, or progress spark.
- Slight upward motion may be introduced from the first **n** toward the **i**.

### 4.3 Brand Symbol

Recommended symbol name:

> **The Nummi Coin**

Concept:

- a rounded coin or seed shape;
- a simple smiling coin face;
- one small sprout or upward spark;
- usable independently as an app icon and mascot badge.

The symbol should communicate:

- money;
- growth;
- friendliness;
- learning;
- progress.

### 4.4 Logo Lockups

Approved configurations:

1. **Horizontal**
   - Symbol + `nummi`
   - Default for websites, headers, and school materials.

2. **Stacked**
   - Symbol above `nummi`
   - Used in posters, splash screens, and presentations.

3. **Wordmark only**
   - Used in compact UI spaces.

4. **Symbol only**
   - App icon, avatar, favicon, badge, and loading animation.

5. **Formal lockup**
   - `NUMMI`
   - `Numbuhi Uang lewat Misi, Manajemen, dan Investasi`

### 4.5 Clear Space

Use the height of the lowercase **n** as the minimum clear space around the logo.

No text, illustration, border, or device edge may enter this area.

### 4.6 Minimum Size

| Usage | Minimum |
|---|---:|
| Digital wordmark | 96 px wide |
| Symbol | 24 px |
| Printed wordmark | 28 mm wide |
| Printed symbol | 8 mm |

### 4.7 Logo Misuse

Do not:

- stretch or compress the logo;
- rotate the wordmark;
- use unapproved gradients;
- add heavy shadows;
- outline the wordmark;
- place it on a visually noisy background;
- recolor individual letters randomly;
- turn the logo into a pig or piggy-bank illustration;
- replace the coin dot with unrelated emoji.

## 7. Illustration System

## 7.1 Illustration Style

Nummi illustrations should be:

- rounded;
- soft-edged;
- colorful but controlled;
- simple enough to understand instantly;
- expressive without becoming visually noisy;
- composed from large shapes;
- lightly textured;
- emotionally warm.

### Visual Construction

- Bold primary silhouette.
- Minimal internal detail.
- Rounded outlines.
- Soft shadow or layered paper-cut depth.
- Decorative stars, leaves, coins, clouds, and spark shapes.
- No photorealistic money, investment charts, or banking environments for Little.

---

## 7.2 Illustration Worlds

Each financial behavior may have its own visual world.

| Category | Visual World | Example Elements |
|---|---|---|
| Spend | Everyday Town | snack stall, school bag, bus, bookshop |
| Save | Dream Island | mountain, bicycle, treasure map, target flag |
| Give | Kindness Garden | heart flowers, gifts, helping hands |
| Grow | Growth Forest | seed, tree rings, golden leaves, parent guide |
| Missions | Adventure Trail | map, checkpoints, badges, bridges |
| Parent Approval | Family Station | parent character, message bubble, safe box |

### Illustration Rule

Illustration must explain or reinforce the action. It should not exist only as decoration.

## 8. Mascot System

## 8.1 Mascot Role

Recommended mascot name:

> **Nummi**

The mascot is a cute golden kancil / baby mouse deer with a small green sprout and purple Nummi scarf.

The mascot acts as:

- learning companion;
- decision guide;
- narrator;
- celebration partner;
- gentle reminder;
- bridge between child and parent.

The mascot is not:

- a financial advisor;
- a judge;
- a salesperson;
- a reward machine.

Approved direction:

## 8.3 Mascot States

Create at least these core poses:

1. Welcome
2. Thinking
3. Explaining
4. Celebrating
5. Waiting for parent
6. Encouraging after a mistake
7. Goal achieved
8. Mission unlocked
9. Money moved
10. Grow or investment explanation

## 8.4 Expression Rules

- Never shame the child.
- Never look angry because a child spent money.
- Warning states should look concerned and helpful.
- Celebrations should reward learning and consistency, not large balances.
- Parent approval states should feel safe, not restrictive.

## 10. Shape and Layout Language

### Core Shapes

- Rounded cards: 20–30 px radius.
- Rounded buttons: 14–20 px radius or capsule.
- Circular coins and badges.
- Soft organic decorative blobs.
- Avoid sharp rectangles and aggressive diagonals.

### Layout Principles

1. One screen, one primary action.
2. Important money values remain easy to scan.
3. Illustration and action should work together.
4. Decorative elements must not reduce readability.
5. Little uses fewer, larger components.
6. Middle uses goals, cards, and visual progress.
7. Teen uses calmer density and more structured information.

### Touch Targets

| Tier | Minimum |
|---|---:|
| Little | 54 px |
| Middle | 48 px |
| Teen | 46 px |

## 11. Motion System

Motion should communicate cause and effect.

### Motion Categories

| Motion | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Micro | Button feedback and selection |
| Transfer | Shows money moving between purposes |
| Progress | Shows a goal getting closer |
| Approval | Shows request status changing |
| Celebration | Marks learning milestones |
| Mascot | Adds personality and guidance |

### Timing

| Motion | Duration |
|---|---:|
| Micro | 120–160 ms |
| Standard transition | 220–300 ms |
| Money movement | 450–600 ms |
| Celebration | 600–900 ms |

### Motion Rules

- Money should visibly travel from source to destination.
- Total balance must not animate in a way that implies money was created.
- Grow and Harvest need slower, more deliberate motion.
- Provide reduced-motion support.
- Avoid endless bouncing or attention-demanding animation.

## 17. Accessibility

Nummi must be usable by early readers and children with different needs.

### Requirements

- WCAG-compliant contrast for functional text.
- Do not rely on color alone.
- Minimum touch targets by tier.
- Read-aloud option for Little.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Clear focus states.
- Simple sentence structure.
- Consistent category placement.
- No flashing animation.
- Numbers should be grouped clearly.
- Indonesian currency formatting should be consistent.

### Rupiah Format

Recommended:

- `Rp50.000`
- `Rp1.250.000`

Do not mix:

- `Rp 50,000`
- `IDR 50K`
- `50 ribu rupiah`

within the same interface unless appropriate to a specific educational explanation.

---

## 18. Co-Branding Rules

For school or institutional partnerships:

- Nummi remains visually primary or equal.
- Partner logo must not replace the Nummi wordmark.
- Maintain clear space between logos.
- Use a vertical divider for horizontal lockups.
- Partner colors may appear in event materials but must not alter category semantics.
- Product UI remains Nummi-led.
- Use the phrase:

> **Nummi for Schools, in collaboration with [Partner]**

For parent or bank partnerships, avoid visual treatment that makes Nummi appear to hold real money unless legally and operationally accurate.

---

## 19. Brand Do and Don’t

### Do

- Show children making choices.
- Celebrate habits and learning.
- Use friendly, precise financial language.
- Keep category colors consistent.
- Use illustration to explain.
- Make parents visible as collaborators.
- Connect missions to real actions.
- Show progress without pressure.

### Don’t

- Promise wealth.
- Glorify excessive saving.
- Shame spending.
- Use investment returns as guaranteed rewards.
- Make Nummi look like a casino or trading app.
- Use rankings based on balance.
- Use punitive streak loss.
- Hide that real money is held or managed by parents.
- Turn every screen into a game.
